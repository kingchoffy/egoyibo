More than
just music

Egoyibo Sounds is a Highlife/Rap and secular musician originally from Ideato Local Government, Imo State, Nigeria.

He grew up in Warri and fell in love with music at a very early age.

He started his career with his first song, a refix of “Chop My Money”, originally by the legendary group P-Square.

After his first record, his passion for music grew stronger. He released his first single, “Loving This Girl”, in 2013, produced by Firsty Production.

He started performing at birthday parties and local shows around the city of Warri.

Egoyibo Sounds was inspired early by songwriter and singer Bym Zeal, his elder brother, and by Eastern legend Phyno, “the Playmaker”.

Since starting out, he has released songs and an EP titled “Ego of Africa”, featuring the legendary Erigga “Paparboi” on the track “Uwoke Oma”.

His singles include “Ama Ndi Aneze” (Heart of Man), “Ghetto Time”, “Still Arise” and many others, reflecting his journey and the strength of standing strong.

Today, Egoyibo Sounds is focused on creating global records, albums and collaborations, touring around the globe, and building a bigger audience and fan base.

His favorite words remain “Otwá Kor Di Arabanko”.
